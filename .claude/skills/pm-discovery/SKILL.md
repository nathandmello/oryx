# Frame and validate customer problems

Use [run-discovery](../run-discovery/SKILL.md) when the request is part of formal initiative discovery.

1. Follow [the session steps](../../../playbook/working.md#run-a-workflow). Read [Problem](../../../playbook/sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-4-problem) and [Data & Insights](../../../playbook/sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-5-data--insights).
2. Read the related goal, product context, and evidence. Open the existing record or use the [problem template](../../../templates/problem.md).
3. Translate incoming feature requests back into problems. Use the source statement for customer, task, situation, and observable signal.
4. Put retrievable evidence and sourced verbatims under Know. Put disputed or untested claims under Think and unanswered questions under Curious.
5. Use quantitative evidence for scale and change. Test significance when the design permits. Use qualitative evidence for behaviour and motivation.
6. Mark the belief or unknown that costs most if wrong. Match the product or market question to the source's research matrix.
7. Keep counterevidence, dates, and sampling limits beside the claim. Use the [confidence scale](../../../playbook/sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#whats-your-confidence).
8. State what the evidence supports and what remains open. Follow [the checkpoint steps](../../../playbook/working.md#checkpoint).
