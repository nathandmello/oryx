# Align goals and outcomes

1. Follow [the session steps](../../../playbook/working.md#run-a-workflow). Read [Goals Alignment](../../../playbook/sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-3-goals-alignment).
2. Find the relevant context, authoritative goal, and owner. Open the existing record or use the [goal template](../../../templates/goal.md).
3. Capture stakeholder inputs first. Use three visible columns for Solutions, Customer outcomes, and Organisation objectives.
4. Trace each proposed solution back to its problem and intended outcome. Move misplaced features or metrics to their useful place.
5. Link organisation, product, initiative, and feature goals where applicable. Show the customer outcome and which metric measures it.
6. Define the metric, baseline, target, time horizon, and guardrails. State measurement gaps and link the sources.
   Use the [metric registry](../../../playbook/metrics.md) for the effective calculation and population.
7. When direction is unclear, ask why the organisation funds the team. Use an example to discuss a customer outcome that matters.
8. Record the provisional contribution and its owner. Keep unconfirmed targets and unresolved baselines visible.
9. Record the PM's accepted decisions and flag affected work. Follow [the checkpoint steps](../../../playbook/working.md#checkpoint).
