# Review existing context

1. Follow [the session steps](../../../playbook/working.md#run-a-workflow). Read [the context rules](../../../playbook/context.md).
2. Read the existing company, product, or initiative brief, its sources, and its parent context when present.
3. Check the scope, authority, and date of each source relevant to the review. Surface conflicting claims and missing evidence.
4. Use [ingest-context](../ingest-context/SKILL.md) for requested capture, updates, or reconciliation. Follow its routing and write checks.
5. Record the review date only after the review. Check that the maintainer and review trigger still fit the brief.
6. Flag affected goals, strategy, or other linked work. Record the effect of unresolved gaps.
7. Follow [the checkpoint steps](../../../playbook/working.md#checkpoint). Keep the brief in draft while required controls are missing.

When material evidence is unavailable, record the affected claim and the evidence needed. Keep the existing review date while that review is incomplete.
