# Product strategy framework

Use [Product Strategy Mindset](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md) as the complete method. Read the relevant source section before applying the Hub workflow.

| Work | Original section | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Choose what to pursue and make the argument | [Product Strategy](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-1-product-strategy) and [Strategy Template](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-2-strategy-template) | [pm-strategy](../.agents/skills/pm-strategy/SKILL.md) and [strategy template](../templates/strategy.md) |
| Align business goals, customer outcomes, and measures | [Goals Alignment](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-3-goals-alignment) | [pm-goals](../.agents/skills/pm-goals/SKILL.md) and [goal template](../templates/goal.md) |
| Frame a problem and choose research | [Problem](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-4-problem) and [Data & Insights](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-5-data--insights) | [pm-discovery](../.agents/skills/pm-discovery/SKILL.md) or [run-discovery](../.agents/skills/run-discovery/SKILL.md) for a registered initiative |
| Test an assumption and assess confidence | [Hypothesis](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-6-hypothesis) | [pm-experiment](../.agents/skills/pm-experiment/SKILL.md) or [run-discovery](../.agents/skills/run-discovery/SKILL.md) for signed discovery |
| Rank outcomes by confidence in creating value | [Iterations](sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-7-iterations) | [pm-roadmap](../.agents/skills/pm-roadmap/SKILL.md) for standalone work or [prioritize-roadmap](../.agents/skills/prioritize-roadmap/SKILL.md) for registered initiatives |

Use the [brief-review checks](brief-review.md) when judging a decision. Use [the opportunity workflow](opportunities.md) for signed initiative decisions.
