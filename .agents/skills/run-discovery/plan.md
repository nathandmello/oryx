# Plan the investigation

**Entry condition.** A registered initiative has an evaluated problem and a decision that depends on missing evidence.

Ask which decision would change if the hypothesis failed. Choose a method that can distinguish the competing explanations.
For example, a usability session can reveal friction. A claim about willingness to pay needs evidence about a purchase.
Offer a test that fits the team's access to customers. The PM chooses the risk to test and the decision criterion.

1. Read company and product context, then the evidence for areas explicitly linked to the initiative. Identify the decision-critical uncertainty.
2. Open the initiative’s Discovery Brief. If it needs a draft, use the [draft command](../../../templates/README.md#create-a-draft) with the registered initiative ID.
3. Propose decision-critical `hypothesis` records. Name the risk and cite the basis for each. [Match the test to the claim](../../../playbook/brief-review.md#match-the-test-to-the-hypothesis).
4. Describe the timebox, decision, methods, exit criteria, reviewers, and capacity in the brief.
5. Use `initiative propose` to validate `start-discovery` with a review date. Ask the PM to review the brief and approve.
6. Before research begins, read the signed authorization. Use the documented timebox and methods.

Keep the template’s section headings. Use `###` for additional subsections. Indent literal headings inside code samples.

For a new timebox, preserve previous evidence and obtain a new signed start decision. Customer contact and external research require the applicable permission.
