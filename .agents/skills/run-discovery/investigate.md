# Investigate and conclude

**Entry condition.** The signed record authorizes the current timebox.

Keep approved plan citations in Sources. Cite new observations in Findings and recommendation.
If plan sources change, obtain a fresh signed `start-discovery` decision. Older timeboxes need this review before `prepare-brief`.

1. Gather approved evidence. Link results to the initiative and protect customer data.
2. Resume the metric-linked draft from `initiative show`, or start one with `initiative draft-experiment`.
3. Fill known details. [Review the hypothesis, measure, and criterion](../../../playbook/brief-review.md#match-the-test-to-the-hypothesis). Propose `approve-experiment` when ready. Obtain signed approval before running the test.
4. Propose `result` with its experiment ID, observed date, conclusion, and source. Preserve inconclusive and contradictory results.
5. Propose `assess` for every decision-critical hypothesis. Cite evidence and limits. Give a 0–10 judgment of confidence in the specific claim.
6. Review findings against learning goals and the four risks. Record the recommendation and residual risk in the Discovery Brief.
7. Complete the Discovery Brief. Propose `prepare-brief`, a new timebox, deferral, or stop as appropriate.

Wait for the PM's signed decision. Use [define-initiative](../define-initiative/SKILL.md) when the PM chooses definition.
