# Investigate and conclude

**Entry condition.** The register shows PM approval for the current timebox.

Keep approved plan citations in Sources. Cite new observations in Findings and recommendation.
If plan sources change, obtain fresh PM approval for `start-discovery`. Older timeboxes need this review before `prepare-brief`.

1. Gather approved evidence. Link results to the initiative and protect customer data.
2. Resume the metric-linked draft from `initiative show`, or start one with `initiative draft-experiment`.
3. Fill known details. [Review the hypothesis, measure, and criterion](../../../playbook/brief-review.md#match-the-test-to-the-hypothesis). Propose `approve-experiment` when ready. Obtain PM approval before running the test.
4. Propose `result` with its experiment ID, observed date, conclusion, and source. Preserve inconclusive and contradictory results.
5. Propose `assess` for every decision-critical hypothesis. Cite evidence and limits. Ask for the PM's 0–10 confidence in each claim.
6. Review findings against learning goals and the four risks. Attribute the recommendation to the PM. Keep unresolved risks visible.
7. Complete the Discovery Brief. Propose `prepare-brief`, a new timebox, deferral, or stop as appropriate.

Wait for the PM's recorded decision. Use [define-initiative](../define-initiative/SKILL.md) when the PM chooses definition.
