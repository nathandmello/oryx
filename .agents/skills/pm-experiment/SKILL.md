# Design and interpret experiments

Use [run-discovery](../run-discovery/SKILL.md) for experiments governed by an initiative's signed discovery plan.

1. Follow [the session steps](../../../playbook/working.md#run-a-workflow). Read [Hypothesis](../../../playbook/sources/product-strategy-mindset.md#section-6-hypothesis).
2. Link the problem, intended outcome, and riskiest assumption. Open the existing record or use the [experiment template](../../../templates/experiment.md).
3. Use the four test-matrix statements. Express the hypothesis as an if–then claim about behaviour. Rate criticality low, medium, or high.
4. Choose the smallest credible test. Weigh cost, confidence in the data, and time to measure. Aim for a fortnight when appropriate.
5. Record the measure, guardrails, decision criterion, and inconclusive conditions before testing. Obtain any authorisation the test requires.
6. For an existing result, recover the original criterion and its timing. Mark it unknown if no prior record supports it.
7. Preserve dated amendments and their reasons. Label any criterion first proposed after results as retrospective.
8. Record the observation and classify the result against the original criterion as supported, contradicted, or inconclusive.
   If the original criterion is unknown, record the observation and leave that decision inconclusive.
9. Apply the source's confidence scale to the evidence collected. Record the limits that affect the result.
10. Follow [the checkpoint steps](../../../playbook/working.md#checkpoint). Link the result and any accepted decision to affected work.
